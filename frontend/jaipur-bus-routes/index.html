Jaipur City Bus Routes and Stops

Looking for Jaipur bus routes, JCTSL bus stops or buses near your location? Jaipur Bus Live brings available city bus information and map-based tracking tools together in one place.

Search Jaipur bus routes and stops

Open the app to search available routes, explore bus stops and view bus information provided by the connected service. The routes and live data shown may change depending on data availability.

Jaipur bus timings and fares

Bus timings, route operation and fares can change. Confirm current service details with official transport sources before travelling; this guide does not promise a fixed fare or arrival time.

Can I track a JCTSL bus live?

When GPS data is available, the app can display bus information on a map. A map location is not a guaranteed arrival-time estimate.
