Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Verified transit facts, schedules, and fare rules for Jaipur Metro (JMRC Pink Line).

JMRC Guide
What are the operating timings of Jaipur Metro Pink Line?

Jaipur Metro operates daily from 06:20 AM to 09:50 PM. Peak frequency is every 10–12 minutes, and off-peak frequency is every 15 minutes between Mansarovar and Badi Chaupar.

How many stations are there on the Jaipur Metro Pink Line?

The Jaipur Metro Pink Line consists of 11 stations over 11.97 km: Mansarovar, New Aatish Market, Vivek Vihar, Shyam Nagar, Ram Nagar, Civil Lines, Railway Station, Sindhi Camp, Chandpole, Chhoti Chaupar, and Badi Chaupar (8 elevated and 3 underground stations).

What is the ticket fare for Jaipur Metro?

Jaipur Metro single token fares range from ₹6 for 1–2 stations, ₹12 for 3–5 stations, ₹18 for 6–8 stations, up to ₹22 for 9 or more stations. JMRC Smart Card users receive an automatic 10% discount on every journey.

Where can I interchange between Jaipur Metro, Indian Railways, and Bus Stand?

Railway Station metro station provides direct access to Jaipur Junction Railway Station, while Sindhi Camp metro station connects directly to Sindhi Camp Central Bus Stand.

How long does it take to travel the entire Jaipur Metro Pink Line?

The end-to-end journey from Mansarovar to Badi Chaupar covers 11.97 km and takes approximately 24 to 34 minutes.

What is the Jaipur Metro route between Mansarovar, Railway Station, and Chandpole?

The Jaipur Metro Pink Line connects Mansarovar directly to Railway Station (Jaipur Junction) and Chandpole in approximately 18–20 minutes, serving major commercial, residential, and transit hubs across Jaipur with trains every 10–15 minutes.

How can I find the nearest Jaipur Metro station and plan my journey?

Use the Jaipur Metro Route Planner on Jaipur Bus Live to select any boarding station across all 11 Pink Line stations from Mansarovar to Badi Chaupar. View station details, interchanges with Indian Railways or Sindhi Camp bus stand, journey durations, and token or smart card fares.
